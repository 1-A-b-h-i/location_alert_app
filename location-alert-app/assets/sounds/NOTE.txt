ALARM SOUND PLACEHOLDER

To use a real alarm sound:
1. Download an alarm or alert sound file in MP3 format
2. Rename it to "alarm.mp3"
3. Place it in this directory (assets/sounds/)

The sound should be:
- Clear and distinctive
- Not too long (ideally 1-3 seconds for a repeating sound)
- Appropriate volume level

Royalty-free sound effects can be found on websites like:
- soundsnap.com
- freesound.org
- zapsplat.com

Note: For testing purposes, you may want to use a gentler sound to avoid startling yourself during development.
